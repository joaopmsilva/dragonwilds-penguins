    Penguin Hunting

    Update 1.0.2 let the Killer Gentoo Penguins (the KGP) loose across Ashenfall, disguised as everyday objects. Spot one, interact with it, and it flees and leaves you a Penguin Coin. Spend your coins on penguin armour, trinkets and decorations at Façade's Front.

    18out at once: 3 in each of 6 regions
    7in-game days until they move

    1. Start the hunt at Façade's Front

    Head to the Adventurer's Guild ruins in Bramblemead Valley, east of Bramblemead Village. Walk past Wormelbow's goblin cage at the entrance and the hidden reliquary, and talk to the door on the far side: that's Fishy Façade, a KGP informant pretending to be a door.

    Show the shop on the map

    Fishy Façade: Password?
    You pick one: "The Pidgeon Flies Left in Winter?", "Err... Lemon?" or "Password1234?"
    Fishy Façade: What? Huh? That's not a thing. Who recruited you? Terry? I bet it was Terry...

    Any answer works. The door briefs you on the penguin menace anyway and the Penguin Hunting quest starts. It never ends ("It shall never be over"), so you can keep hunting. After the first talk, choose "Just open the shop." to go straight to the shop.

    2. Find the penguins

    1. Three penguins per region are out at a time, in Brynmoor, Ghornfell, Fellhollow, Scorned Wilderness, Dowdun Reach and Umbral Sands.
    2. They hide as a box, pot, barrel or sack. In Umbral Sands they're desert pots or rugs. Look for one that seems out of place.
    3. Interact with it. It reveals itself, flees, and you get 1 Penguin Coin.
    4. Each penguin counts once per character. Your friends can find the same penguin for their own coin, so hunting together works.
    5. Every 7 in-game days the penguins move to new spots, and the hunt starts over.

    Each cycle the game fills 3 of a region's spots: in Brynmoor that's 3 of its 17, in Umbral Sands 3 of its 64. Use the map to work through a region's spots and tick off the ones you've checked. Your ticks stay in this browser; clear them when a new cycle starts.

    3. Spend your coins

    Trade Penguin Coins at Façade's Front. Everything is open from the start: no reputation and no level needed. Gear comes as an item you use to learn its recipe, then craft. Decorations come as building plans.

    5. Collect Agent Ravioli's reports

    The penguins keep notes on you. The journal has a Penguin Report for each region, signed by Agent Ravioli: Brynmoor, Ghornfell, Fellhollow, Whispering Swamp, Dowdun Reach, Scorned Wilderness and Umbral Sands. There's also one for Disgleiried, an elven land that isn't in the game yet, so expect penguins to follow you there.

    Tips

    • Hunt with friends: everyone in the group gets a coin from the same penguin.
    • Penguins in Scorned Wilderness are in its own zone, so pick it in the map's region list.
    • Share a spot with the Copy link button in its pop-up.
    • The whole shop costs 359 coins, which is 20 cycles at 18 penguins each. The KGP Hat and the cape are the big ones at 30 coins each.
